Before You Start

This tool helps you calculate total recipe cost, cost per portion, and an ideal selling price for each of your 3 standardized recipes, scaled to 50 servings. Your entries are saved automatically in this browser as you work.

Tip: Have your standardized recipes and ingredient prices (grocery ads, apps, or your own shopper knowledge) ready before you begin. You'll enter the original recipe yield and each ingredient's cost for that yield — the tool scales everything to 50 servings for you.

Part I: Determine Recipe Cost

Enter each recipe's original yield and ingredients. Costs scale automatically to the 50-serving target.

Recipe (Total) Cost = ∑ (ingredient cost, scaled)
Portion Cost = Total Recipe Cost ÷ Servings (50)

Part II: Calculate Selling Price

Default desired food cost percentage is 33.3%, as specified in the assignment. You may adjust it if your instructor directs otherwise.

Portion Cost = Total Food Cost ÷ Servings
Selling Price = Food Cost (per portion) ÷ Desired Food Cost %

Part III: Documentation and Upload

Add any notes or assumptions (rounding, market prices, substitutions), then review and export your complete write-up to submit as your assignment file.
